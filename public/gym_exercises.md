# Gym Exercises Library

## Exercise: Barbell Bench Press
### Body Part: Chest
### Equipment: Barbell, Bench
### Description: Fundamental horizontal pressing exercise to overload the pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, and triceps.
### Cues: Retract scapulae, unrack over shoulders, touch lower sternum, and press with leg drive.

## Exercise: Incline Dumbbell Press
### Body Part: Chest
### Equipment: Dumbbells, Bench
### Description: Targets the clavicular head (upper chest) and anterior deltoids with a greater range of motion.
### Cues: Set bench angle to 30 degrees, press upward in an arch without clacking dumbbells at the top.

## Exercise: Cable Pectoral Flyes
### Body Part: Chest
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Constant-tension isolation exercise targeting peak contraction in the chest.
### Cues: Keep a slight bend in the elbows and squeeze the chest at full horizontal adduction.

## Exercise: Barbell Back Squat
### Body Part: Legs
### Equipment: Barbell, Squat Rack
### Description: Compound lower-body builder targeting quadriceps, glutes, and spinal erectors.
### Cues: Brace core, break at hips and knees simultaneously, keep knees tracking over toes.

## Exercise: Romanian Deadlift
### Body Part: Legs
### Equipment: Barbell, Dumbbells
### Description: Hip-hinge movement that heavily overloads the hamstrings and glutes through eccentric stretch.
### Cues: Maintain a flat back, push hips back until hamstrings stretch, drive hips forward to lockout.

## Exercise: Leg Press
### Body Part: Legs
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Machine-based quad and glute builder that allows high loading without axial spine loading.
### Cues: Place feet shoulder-width apart, lower sled until knees form 90 degrees, press through heels without locking knees.

## Exercise: Barbell Bent-Over Row
### Body Part: Back
### Equipment: Barbell
### Description: Heavy compound pulling movement targeting latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, and mid-trapezius.
### Cues: Hinge at 45 degrees, pull barbell to lower chest/belly button, squeeze shoulder blades together.

## Exercise: Lat Pulldown
### Body Part: Back
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Vertical pulling movement targeting lat width and upper back thickness.
### Cues: Pull bar down to upper chest while depressing shoulder blades, avoid excessive leaning back.

## Exercise: Dumbbell Single-Arm Row
### Body Part: Back
### Equipment: Dumbbells, Bench
### Description: Unilateral row for isolating latissimus dorsi and correcting pulling imbalances.
### Cues: Support body on bench, pull dumbbell to hip pocket while keeping torso parallel to floor.

## Exercise: Cable Triceps Pushdown
### Body Part: Triceps
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Isolates the lateral and medial heads of the triceps with continuous cable tension.
### Cues: Pin elbows to ribcage, extend arms straight down, flared or rope extension at the bottom.

## Exercise: Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension
### Body Part: Triceps
### Equipment: Dumbbells
### Description: Stretches and overloads the long head of the triceps in an overhead shoulder-flexed position.
### Cues: Keep upper arms vertical near ears, lower dumbbell behind head, extend fully to lockout.

## Exercise: Dumbbell Bicep Curls
### Body Part: Bicep
### Equipment: Dumbbells
### Description: Classic bicep isolation exercise for elbow flexor hypertrophy.
### Cues: Keep elbows stationary at sides, supinate wrist as dumbbell rises, lower under full control.

## Exercise: Cable Hammer Curls
### Body Part: Bicep
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Targets the brachialis and brachioradialis with a neutral grip for arm thickness.
### Cues: Attach rope to lower cable, maintain neutral thumbs-up grip, curl toward shoulders.

## Exercise: Cable Woodchoppers
### Body Part: Core
### Equipment: Cable Machine
### Description: Rotational core exercise for building oblique strength and anti-rotational stability.
### Cues: Rotate through core while keeping arms relatively straight, drive diagonally across body.

## Exercise: Hanging Leg Raises
### Body Part: Core
### Equipment: Pull-Up Bar
### Description: Advanced abdominal compression movement targeting lower abdominals and hip flexors.
### Cues: Hang from bar, tilt pelvis, pull knees or toes up to bar height without swinging momentum.
